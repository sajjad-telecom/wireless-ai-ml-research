
# Research Summary

## Paper Title
A Comprehensive Survey of Machine Learning Applied to Resource Allocation in Wireless Communications

## Publication Details
- Journal: IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials
- Year: 2025
- DOI: 10.1109/COMST.2025.3552370
- Authors: Pivoto et al.

## Abstract Summary
This paper reviews the application of Machine Learning techniques to resource allocation in wireless communication systems.

It explores how AI-driven approaches can improve network efficiency, optimize resource utilization, reduce latency and congestion, and increase network capacity.

## Key Research Areas
- Machine Learning for Wireless Networks
- Intelligent Resource Allocation
- Network Optimization
- 5G and 6G Communications
- Internet of Things (IoT)
- Energy Efficiency

## Research Objectives
1. Understand ML techniques for wireless resource allocation.
2. Explore intelligent network management.
3. Identify current limitations and research challenges.
4. Investigate future AI-driven wireless networks.
